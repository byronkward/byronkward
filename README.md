## Background

Founded Action Human Technologies, LLC in 1986. 35+ years in executive search, 10+ years hands-on data science, and 4 years in AI. Founder and President and CEO of Central Coast Minority Enterprise Resource Affiliates (CCMERA), a regional 501(c)(3) community and economic empowerment organization. Co-Founder, Previous Chair, and Director at Community Advocacy Coalition of Ventura County, a local social justice non-profit [501(c)(3)] and community FM radio and streaming station. Disabled U.S. Navy veteran and Central State University alumnus. Based in Port Hueneme, California.
